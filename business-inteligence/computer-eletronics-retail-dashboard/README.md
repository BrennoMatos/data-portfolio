# 🖥️ Computer Electronics Retail Dashboard

A one-page executive Business Intelligence dashboard developed in **Power BI** for analyzing the performance of a Brazilian computer and electronics retailer.

The project connects directly to a **PostgreSQL** database and provides an executive overview of sales performance, customers, suppliers, products, and regional distribution through interactive visualizations.

---

# 📸 Dashboard Preview


# 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard was designed to provide business stakeholders with a quick and comprehensive overview of retail performance.

The solution focuses on transforming raw transactional data into meaningful business insights through interactive KPIs and visual analytics.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor total revenue performance
- Track customer purchasing behavior
- Identify best-selling products
- Evaluate supplier contribution
- Analyze sales by Brazilian states
- Support executive decision making

---

# 📊 Dashboard Features

## Executive KPIs

- 💰 Total Revenue
- 📦 Total Orders
- 👥 Active Customers
- 🎫 Average Ticket

---

## Interactive Filters

Users can filter the dashboard by:

- Date
- Product
- Supplier
- Region

---

## Visualizations

### Revenue Trend

Displays revenue evolution over time.

---

### Revenue by Product

Ranks products according to total revenue generated.

---

### Top Suppliers

Shows suppliers responsible for the highest sales volume.

---

### Sales by State

Interactive map showing sales distribution across Brazil.

---

### Top Customers

Identifies the customers generating the highest revenue.

---

### Top Selling Products

Displays products with the highest quantity sold.
